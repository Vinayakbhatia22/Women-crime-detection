The 2013 Nirbhaya Landmark Effect

In 2013, reported crimes jumped by an unprecedented +63,419 cases (+27.0% YoY). Assault on women rose +56.0%, kidnapping rose +35.6%, and rape jumped +35.2%. This reflected the enactment of the Criminal Law (Amendment) Act 2013, making FIR registration mandatory and giving women greater confidence to report.

Anomaly Analysis

The 2020 COVID-19 Lockdown Paradox

Total reported cases plunged by -31,531 cases (-9.5%) in 2020 due to physical mobility restrictions and reduced police station access. However, domestic distress helpline calls surged, leading to a violent rebound in 2021 to a record high of 348,092 cases (+15.2%).

Data Integrity Alert

NCRB Dataset Realignment Notice

In this scraped dataset, 2011 AoW was recorded as 0 across all states. Additionally, when Jammu & Kashmir was reorganized into a UT in 2019, raw rows in 2020–2021 experienced an off-by-one index shift. The dashboard gracefully handles baseline normalizations to ensure accurate figures.
